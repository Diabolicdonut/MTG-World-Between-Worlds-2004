1 · Cover and deck identity

Blue-White Cloudpost Control

U/W Control combines the Onslaught white-control endgame—Eternal Dragon, Exalted Angel, Decree of Justice, Wrath, Akroma’s Vengeance—with a blue permission and draw package tuned for Mirrodin artifacts.

Nassif’s list is not merely “counterspell plus Wrath.” Annul and Stifle interact with Affinity and KCI at unusual angles; Thirst for Knowledge turns Wayfarer’s Bauble or redundant artifacts into cards; Cloudpost and Temple let the deck cast expensive answers while retaining counter mana.

Answers span zones

Counters stop engines, sweepers clear boards, Stifle hits abilities, and Decree wins through cycling.

Finishers resist ordinary removal

Eternal Dragon returns, Decree cycles at instant speed, and Exalted Angel stabilizes life immediately.

Artifacts improve control mana

Bauble fixes and accelerates; Relic Barrier taps opposing artifacts; Thirst converts artifacts into card advantage.

Soft counters scale with mana denial

Mana Leak and Condescend are strongest before Cloudpost/Temple or land destruction changes the mana race.

2 · What this deck does

The strategic machine

Turns 1–3: trade efficiently

Bauble, Annul, Mana Leak, Relic Barrier, or Condescend prevents the opponent’s engine from snowballing.

Turns 4–6: reset and draw

Wrath or Vengeance clears the board while Thirst and Dragon plainscycling maintain land drops.

Turns 6–9: protect the endgame

Rewind, Decree, Exalted Angel, and Eternal Dragon allow the deck to act while retaining interaction.

Late game: win without exposing a sorcery-speed threat

Cycle Decree at end step, recur Dragon, or morph Angel when the opponent cannot profitably answer it.

3 · History and evolution

How the archetype reached Worlds

Onslaught block

White control acquires recursive finishers

Eternal Dragon and Decree of Justice let control decks build mana and win without many vulnerable creatures.

Mirrodin

Thirst and artifact mana reshape blue-white

Wayfarer’s Bauble, Cloudpost, and artifact interaction give the archetype a new infrastructure.

Darksteel / Skullclamp era

Affinity forces maindeck specialization

Annul, Relic Barrier, Purge, and additional sweepers become necessary rather than optional sideboard tools.

Fifth Dawn

Condescend improves the permission curve

Scry and scalable counters help a deck that must find precise mixtures of lands, sweepers, and answers.

Worlds 2004

Nassif reaches Top 8; Mori goes 5–1

The two lists show Cloudpost/Bauble and conventional-mana/Relic Barrier approaches to the same strategic problem.

October rotation

The Onslaught endgame disappears

Eternal Dragon and Decree rotate, forcing blue-white control to rebuild around Kamigawa finishers and Hinder-style permission.

5 · Mulligans and opening turns

Keep a plan, not a collection of powerful cards

Strong keeps

  • Three lands or two lands plus Bauble, with at least one early interaction spell.
  • Against Affinity, Annul/Mana Leak plus Wrath, Vengeance, or multiple cheap answers.
  • Against Tooth/KCI, permission plus a card-advantage engine and stable mana.
  • Dragon plainscycling plus enough early defense to ensure land drops.

Common mulligans

  • Hands full of Decree, Dragon, Vengeance, and Rewind with no play before turn four.
  • Cloudpost/Temple-heavy hands that cannot cast blue or white spells on curve.
  • One-land Bauble hands without a second mana source or early interaction.
  • Against combo, hands relying only on Wrath and creature removal.

Control mirrorPlainscycle Dragon, hit land drops, and cycle Decree at end step rather than tapping out into Rewind.
6 · Key interactions

Rules and sequencing that define the deck

Cycling Decree of Justice creates a triggered ability that produces Soldiers; ordinary counterspells cannot counter the cycling activation or trigger without a Stifle-like effect.

Eternal Dragon can plainscycle early and return from the graveyard during upkeep later, turning a land-fixing card into a finisher.

Thirst for Knowledge requires discarding an artifact or two cards; Wayfarer’s Bauble and excess Relic Barriers are deliberate fuel.

Cloudpost produces mana for each Locus on the battlefield and counts opposing Cloudposts.

Stifle can counter Ravager modular, Disciple triggers, Myr Incubator activation, Mindslaver activation, cycling triggers, and other activated or triggered abilities.

Exalted Angel can be played face down for three and turned face up later, allowing a threat to enter before six mana.

Akroma’s Vengeance destroys artifacts and enchantments as well as creatures, making it a vital catch-all against Slide, Plating, and artifact mana.

7 · How to pilot the deck

Decision rules for real games

Land sequencing is the deck. Colored spells, Cloudpost scaling, Temple thresholds, and leaving counter mana all compete for the same early turns.

Counter engines rather than replaceable bodies. Ravager, Plating, Slide, Ironworks, Tooth, and Mindslaver create more future cards than ordinary creatures.

Do not Wrath merely because the board is nonempty. Life total, Disciple triggers, Nexus, and the opponent’s ability to rebuild determine the correct turn.

Use Thirst before the critical turn when possible, but preserve an artifact to discard so the draw spell remains true card advantage.

Cycle Decree during the opponent’s end step after they commit mana, then untap with countermagic and a clock.

Eternal Dragon is not always meant to be cast. Repeated plainscycling and recursion may be the correct long-game engine.

Against land destruction, prioritize basics and Bauble; Cloudpost and Temple are powerful only if the deck survives to use them.

8 · Sideboarding principles

Change roles without dismantling the engine

Purge efficiently answers red or black threats such as Disciple, Atog, Goblins, and some red finishers.

Pacifism gives cheap creature containment when Wrath is too slow, though sacrifice outlets and bounce reduce reliability.

Relic Barrier taps Plating carriers, artifact lands before mana use, Ironworks pieces, or opposing mana artifacts.

Scrabbling Claws disrupts Eternal Witness/Dragon recursion and cycles when graveyards do not matter.

Stifle is a broad metagame card, not only combo hate; select targets based on how much future value the ability creates.

March of the Machines from Mori’s sideboard can turn artifact lands into 0/0 creatures and become a transformational anti-Affinity plan.

10 · Historical notes and sources

What the archetype teaches

Nassif’s deck is a model of control design under extreme metagame pressure. Its narrow-looking cards—Annul, Stifle, Bauble—are selected because they overlap across Affinity, KCI, Tooth, Slide, and the control mirror.
