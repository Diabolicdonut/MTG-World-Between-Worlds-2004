2004 Standard environment guide

U.S. Nationals 2004

The final major weekend before the Skullclamp ban: Elf and Nail wins over a field of Clamp engines and dedicated anti-artifact control.

Table of contentsEvent overviewTop finishersExact decklistsHistorical meaning
1 · Event

Event overview

U.S. Nationals began June 18 with Skullclamp still legal; the ban took effect June 20. Craig Krempels won with Elf and Nail, proving that Clamp’s most powerful application extended beyond Affinity and Goblins.
