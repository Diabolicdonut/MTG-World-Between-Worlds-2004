Smallest convincing version

For a six-deck box, use Nuijten Slide, Paquette Affinity, Bevand KCI, Zorc Tooth and Nail, Ruel Big Red, and Nassif U/W Control. That set preserves the format's aggro, combo, ramp, tap-out control, permission, and anti-artifact dynamics.

Worlds 2004 Standard

Tokens and counters

You need Myr tokens for Myr Incubator, Soldier tokens for Decree of Justice, +1/+1 counters for modular, charge counters for Pentad Prism and Oblivion Stone, and ordinary markers for Astral Slide timing.

Worlds 2004 Standard

Historical-play note

Combat damage still used the stack in 2004. Ravager, Atog, Goblin Sledder, Skirk Prospector, and Siege-Gang Commander become stronger when games are played with the period rules. Decide before the gauntlet whether to preserve those rules or use current rules consistently.
