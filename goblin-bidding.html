Goblin Bidding

Scope

Scope of this guide

Worlds-cycle position: This dossier covers the final Odyssey–Onslaught Standard environment at Berlin and the short period immediately afterward, before Mirrodin became legal on October 20, 2003.
1 · Deck identity

Goblin Bidding

Goblin Bidding fused Onslaught tribal aggression with Odyssey graveyard recursion. The deck could win through Goblin Piledriver tempo, grind creature boards with Sparksmith and Sharpshooter, or deliberately fill its graveyard before a one-card rebuild.

2 · Game plan

What this deck does

Develop cheap Goblins and exploit Warchief cost reduction and haste.

Use Sparksmith, Gempalm Incinerator, and Sharpshooter to control creature combat.

Trade creatures or sacrifice them through Sledder and Prospector without fearing permanent attrition.

Resolve Patriarch’s Bidding to return a lethal board, often with immediate haste from Warchief.

3 · Development

History and evolution

Onslaught and Legions

The tribal core arrives

Piledriver, Warchief, Sledder, Sparksmith, Prospector, and Siege-Gang provide speed, removal, mana, and reach.

Odyssey block

Patriarch’s Bidding creates the recursion engine

Black mana and graveyard setup let the deck recover from Wrath and win long games.

Worlds 2003

Two distinct Bidding lists excel

Wolfgang Eder reaches the Top 8; Sylvain Lauriol goes 6–0 using Read the Runes to stock the graveyard and find Bidding.

Mirrodin and Darksteel

The shell evolves rather than disappears

Odyssey rotates, ending Bidding itself, but the Goblin core survives and later adopts Skullclamp before becoming R/G anti-Affinity Goblins at Worlds 2004.

4 · Construction

Key design characteristics

Aggro-combo duality

Opponents must respect ordinary Piledriver attacks and a graveyard-based mass-recursion finish.

Creature utility density

Most creatures are also mana, removal, card selection, haste, or direct damage.

Sacrifice economy

Prospector and Sledder turn creatures into mana or combat advantages, which improves both Sharpshooter chains and Bidding.

Splash flexibility

Black supplies Bidding and removal; the 6–0 version adds blue for Read the Runes and sideboard options.

6 · Opening decisions

Mulligans and opening turns

Keep

  • A red source, a one-drop, and Warchief or Piledriver.
  • Hands with functional black mana and a realistic Bidding path against control.
  • Prospector plus Siege-Gang or a curve that benefits from acceleration.

Send back

  • Hands with multiple five-mana spells and no early board.
  • Black-heavy hands unable to cast early red Goblins.
  • Read the Runes hands without enough creatures or graveyard payoff.

Opening priorities

  1. Choose whether turn-one Prospector is acceleration or a resource to preserve for Bidding math.
  2. Against control, develop enough pressure to demand Wrath without emptying every Goblin from hand.
  3. Sequence Warchief so newly returned or newly cast Goblins create immediate damage.
7 · Rules and sequencing

Key interactions

Patriarch’s Bidding returns all creature cards of the chosen type from every graveyard, so opposing tribal creatures also matter.

Goblin Warchief gives returned Goblins haste after Bidding resolves.

Goblin Sharpshooter untaps whenever a creature dies and can create long damage chains with sacrifice outlets.

Read the Runes can sacrifice permanents instead of discarding, allowing Goblins to enter the graveyard before Bidding.

8 · Play patterns

How to pilot the deck

Count lethal through Warchief and Piledriver before using utility Goblins for removal.

Against sweepers, treat the graveyard as stored battlefield value rather than a loss.

Sparksmith is powerful but life-intensive; stop activating when the race becomes unfavorable.

Bidding is not always the immediate play—sometimes a smaller attack forces a counter or removal spell first.

9 · Post-board roles

Sideboarding principles

Wake

Sulfuric Vortex and Cabal Therapy pressure life gain and permission; Starstorm can reset transformed creature plans.

U/G Madness

Smother and Starstorm contain large creatures; Flaring Pain can break fog or prevention effects.

Reanimator

Coffin Purge and Cabal Therapy disrupt graveyard timing; maintain a fast clock.

Astral Slide

Cleansing effects and graveyard management matter; repeated Slide triggers can remove Warchief before combat.
