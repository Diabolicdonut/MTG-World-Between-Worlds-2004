1 · Cover and deck identity

Green-White Anti-Affinity Control

G/W Control removes the Astral Slide engine and replaces synergy with concentrated card quality: Rampant Growth, Solemn Simulacrum, Eternal Witness, Oxidize, Wrath, Akroma’s Vengeance, Plow Under, Exalted Angel, Eternal Dragon, and Decree of Justice.

The deck’s defining loop is not blink but recursion. Eternal Witness turns every powerful sorcery into a potential second copy, and the mana acceleration ensures that expensive spells can be cast while continuing to develop the board.

No engine tax

Without Slide and a large cycling package, more slots can be direct answers, threats, and Plow Under.

High-impact recursion

Witness makes the best graveyard card the next spell, letting matchup context choose the engine.

Threats stabilize and finish

Exalted Angel gains life; Dragon fixes mana and recurs; Decree produces an instant-speed army.

Mana denial inside control

Plow Under attacks lands and draw steps, functioning as both tempo and soft lock when repeated.

2 · What this deck does

The strategic machine

Turns 1–3: ramp and interact

Rampant Growth, Solemn, and Oxidize build mana while preventing Affinity from snowballing.

Turns 4–6: dominate the battlefield

Wrath/Vengeance reset, Exalted Angel stabilizes life, and Plow Under prevents recovery.

Turns 5–8: recur the best spell

Witness returns Wrath, Plow, Oxidize, Decree, or a threat according to the matchup.

Late game: overwhelm with resilient threats

Dragon recursion and Decree make ordinary one-for-one control plans fail.

3 · History and evolution

How the archetype reached Worlds

Onslaught block

White control supplies the finishers

Eternal Dragon, Exalted Angel, Decree, and Akroma’s Vengeance create a powerful expensive shell.

Mirrodin / Darksteel

Affinity demands maindeck artifact answers

Oxidize and broad sweepers become essential, while Solemn Simulacrum improves mana and attrition.

Fifth Dawn

Eternal Witness completes green control

Witness lets the deck replay Plow Under, Wrath, and artifact hate without needing Astral Slide.

Worlds 2004

Olivier Ruel goes 5–1

The list proves that green-white anti-Affinity control can succeed without the cycling engine.

Rotation

The exact shell dissolves

Onslaught’s Dragon, Decree, Angel, and Vengeance leave, while Witness remains to define later green midrange decks.

5 · Mulligans and opening turns

Keep a plan, not a collection of powerful cards

Strong keeps

  • Two or three lands with both colors and Rampant Growth or Solemn.
  • Against Affinity, Oxidize plus Wrath/Vengeance or Witness to recur interaction.
  • Against slow decks, Plow Under/Witness with enough mana development.
  • Exalted Angel plus ramp and early removal when life stabilization is important.

Common mulligans

  • Hands of expensive fliers and sweepers with no ramp.
  • Temple-heavy hands that do not reach five lands naturally.
  • Against Affinity, hands whose first interaction is turn-four Wrath without life buffer.
  • Hands unable to produce double white on schedule.

6 · Key interactions

Rules and sequencing that define the deck

Plow Under puts two lands on top of their owner’s library, denying both mana and fresh draw steps.

Eternal Witness can return Plow Under repeatedly, creating a soft lock when the opponent cannot add meaningful permanents.

Temple of the False God produces mana only when five or more lands are controlled, making early land sequencing critical.

Exalted Angel’s morph allows it to enter for three mana and turn face up later, often gaining life immediately in combat.

Eternal Dragon plainscycles early and returns from the graveyard later, serving both mana and inevitability roles.

Akroma’s Vengeance destroys artifacts and enchantments, allowing a single spell to answer Affinity boards or Astral Slide engines.

Decree of Justice cycles into Soldiers at instant speed and avoids most permission used on ordinary spells.

7 · How to pilot the deck

Decision rules for real games

Ramp is valuable only if life total and battlefield permit the next expensive spell to matter. Do not ignore lethal pressure to cast Solemn.

Choose Witness targets by role. Against Affinity it is usually Oxidize/Wrath; against control it may be Plow Under or Decree.

Use Plow Under when the opponent is least able to replay the lands—after a sweep, before a key mana threshold, or with Witness ready.

Morph Exalted Angel when the disguise changes combat or countermagic decisions; hard-casting may be safer against removal.

Do not overextend large threats into opposing Wrath. Dragon and Decree provide inevitability without multiple battlefield creatures.

Vengeance is broader than Wrath but more destructive to your own artifacts and enchantments; sequence Solemn and other permanents to extract value first.

Against combo, the deck must create a clock. Repeating Plow is not enough if KCI or Tooth can eventually rebuild and win in one turn.

8 · Sideboarding principles

Change roles without dismantling the engine

Relic Barrier buys repeated tempo against Plating, artifact lands, Ironworks, and mana rocks.

Wing Shards punishes large attack steps and does not target individual protected creatures in the same way as ordinary removal.

Scrabbling Claws checks Witness, Dragon, and graveyard recursion while cycling when unnecessary.

Creeping Mold adds land and permanent destruction against Tooth, Slide, Cloudpost, and March.

Circle of Protection: Red converts red damage decks into long games that favor Dragon/Decree inevitability.

10 · Historical notes and sources

What the archetype teaches

G/W Control is the “good cards” answer to Slide. It shows what the same colors do when the builder rejects a dedicated engine and instead maximizes high-impact spells plus Eternal Witness recursion.
