Scope

Scope of this guide

Worlds-cycle position: This dossier covers the final Odyssey–Onslaught Standard environment at Berlin and the short period immediately afterward, before Mirrodin became legal on October 20, 2003.
1 · Deck identity

U/G Madness

U/G Madness was the era’s premier tempo deck. Wild Mongrel and Aquamoeba transformed discarded cards into immediate battlefield presence, Circular Logic became a one-mana counterspell, and Wonder converted a stalled board into an aerial kill.

2 · Game plan

What this deck does

Establish a discard outlet before loading the graveyard.

Cast Rootwalla and Arrogant Wurm for madness costs while developing pressure.

Protect tempo with Circular Logic and Unsummon.

Use Wonder, Roar of the Wurm, and Deep Analysis to overpower removal-heavy games.

3 · Development

History and evolution

Odyssey

Madness becomes a constructed engine

Wild Mongrel, Basking Rootwalla, Circular Logic, Deep Analysis, and Roar of the Wurm all reward discarding cards.

Onslaught block

The shell gains selective support

Quiet Speculation and sideboard tools improve the graveyard package while the efficient core remains intact.

Worlds 2003

Humpherys reaches the semifinals

U/G Madness is the only non-Wake deck in the Top 4 and a major reason control decks need fogs, sweepers, and instant-speed answers.

October 20, 2003

The engine rotates

Odyssey block leaves Standard, removing nearly the entire archetype at once.

4 · Construction

Key design characteristics

Discard as mana

The deck pays discard costs with cards that want to be discarded, effectively converting one resource into several.

Tempo permission

Circular Logic is strongest while the graveyard grows and the opponent is pressured to tap out.

Graveyard second hand

Deep Analysis, Roar, Wonder, and flashback make the graveyard an extension of the hand.

Low land count

Twenty-two lands are sufficient because Careful Study and discard selection smooth draws, but color sequencing is unforgiving.

6 · Opening decisions

Mulligans and opening turns

Keep

  • Two lands, a discard outlet, and at least one madness or flashback card.
  • Wild Mongrel plus Circular Logic and pressure.
  • Careful Study hands that can discard Wonder, Roar, or excess expensive cards.

Send back

  • Creature hands without Mongrel, Aquamoeba, or Careful Study.
  • One-land hands relying on drawing the second color immediately.
  • Hands full of Wonder and flashback cards with no safe discard outlet.

Opening priorities

  1. Prioritize a discard outlet over maximum turn-one damage.
  2. Sequence lands so Circular Logic can remain available after casting a threat.
  3. Decide whether Wonder belongs in the graveyard now or should remain as a creature against removal-light opponents.
7 · Rules and sequencing

Key interactions

Madness triggers when a card is discarded; the card is exiled briefly and may be cast for its madness cost.

Wild Mongrel changes color until end of turn, which can matter against color-specific removal and protection.

Wonder grants flying only while it is in the graveyard and its controller controls an Island.

Quiet Speculation can find flashback cards such as Roar of the Wurm and Deep Analysis without drawing them first.

8 · Play patterns

How to pilot the deck

Use the minimum discard needed to win combat; every discarded card changes future Logic, flashback, and Wonder decisions.

Against sweepers, preserve Roar or Deep Analysis in the graveyard as the second wave.

Unsummon is often a time-walk against expensive creatures or a way to force through the decisive attack.

Know when to stop developing and hold Circular Logic; the deck loses value if every turn is played at sorcery speed.

9 · Post-board roles

Sideboarding principles

Wake

Add Envelop and Mana Leak; pressure Compulsion and prevent Wrath or Wake from resolving cleanly.

Goblin Bidding

Phantom Centaur and Stupefying Touch help stabilize; graveyard interaction can stop the rebuild.

Reanimator

Unsummon and permission attack the expensive creature, while graveyard cards constrain the engine.

Astral Slide

Nantuko Vigilante and Ray of Revelation answer engines; avoid losing all tempo to repeated Slide triggers.
