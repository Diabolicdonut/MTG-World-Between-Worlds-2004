2004 World Championships

San Francisco, September 1–5: a mixed-format World Championship whose Standard rounds and Top 8 became the defining snapshot of pre-Kamigawa Mirrodin Standard.

Worlds 2004 Standard

The event

The eleventh World Championship brought 304 players from 51 countries to Fort Mason Center. Standard was played on the opening day, followed by Mirrodin–Darksteel–Fifth Dawn Booster Draft and Mirrodin Block Constructed. The elimination rounds returned to Standard.

Worlds 2004 Standard

The final

Julien Nuijten defeated Aeo Paquette 3–1, giving the anti-Affinity Astral Slide deck the title over Affinity itself. Nuijten was fifteen years old and became the youngest World Champion in Magic history.

Worlds 2004 Standard

Standard-day standouts

Five players finished the six Standard rounds at 6–0: Sasha Zorc with Tooth and Nail, Bruno Carvalho with Green-White Slide, Manuel Bevand with KCI, Antoine Ruel with Big Red, and Andrei Häyrynen with Affinity.

Worlds 2004 Standard

Why this event matters

The Top 8 contained three Affinity decks, two Astral Slide decks, Goblins, KCI, and U/W control. The event shows both Affinity's power and the extraordinary amount of deck space the field devoted to beating artifacts.
