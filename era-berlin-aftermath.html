August 11–October 19, 2003

The format immediately after Worlds uses the same core card pool: Eighth Edition, Odyssey, Torment, Judgment, Onslaught, Legions, and Scourge. It is a mature format whose strongest engines are about to rotate.

The environment’s design language

Graveyard as hand

Madness, flashback, Bidding, and reanimation all convert discarded or dead cards into future resources.

Wishboards

Cunning Wish and Burning Wish make sideboards part of game-one deck construction.

Cycling control

Slide, Decree, Renewed Faith, and cycling lands let decks act without committing ordinary spells.

Rotation pressure

Odyssey block is so structurally important that Mirrodin’s entry will erase several top-tier archetypes overnight.

World Between Worlds 2004

October 20: Mirrodin enters

Odyssey, Torment, and Judgment rotate. Wake, Madness, Bidding, and the Berlin Reanimator shell lose their engines. Goblins and Astral Slide retain their Onslaught cores, while Mirrodin introduces artifact lands, affinity, equipment, imprint, entwine, and an entirely new strategic center.
