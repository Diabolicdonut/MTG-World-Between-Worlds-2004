March of the Machines / Worship Control

Damping Matrix disables the artifact format, Worship locks combat, and March turns artifact lands into creatures.

Scope

Scope of this guide

Year position: This is the pre-ban white-blue Matrix/Worship family. The later Worlds U/R March–Obliterate page covers a different March of the Machines strategy.
1 · Deck identity

March of the Machines / Worship Control

The deck attacks Affinity at the rules level. Damping Matrix turns off Ravager, Disciple’s activated support cards, equipment, and many utility artifacts. Worship plus Silver Knight or Pristine Angel can make combat damage unable to end the game. March of the Machines converts artifact lands into fragile creatures.

2 · Game plan

What this deck does

Accelerate with Chrome Mox and establish Damping Matrix.

Use Silver Knight, Wing Shards, and Wrath to control combat.

Lock life total with Worship and a protected creature.

Resolve March to animate artifact lands and destroy them through Wrath or combat.

3 · Development

History and evolution

Early 2004

U/W control seeks artifact-specific answers

Conventional counters and Wraths are sometimes too slow against Ravager.

Darksteel

Damping Matrix becomes central

The deck sacrifices some of its own activated artifacts to disable the opponent’s engine.

Grand Prix Brussels

Mitsios reaches Top 8

The Worship/Matrix shell proves capable against the Clamp field.

U.S. Nationals

Ben Zoz adds March

March provides a proactive way to punish artifact lands.

Post-ban Worlds

The family splits

Traditional U/W returns to Decree and counters, while U/R develops the March–Obliterate lock.

4 · Construction

Key design characteristics

Symmetrical hate

Matrix affects both players, so the deck minimizes activated abilities and relies on static effects and spells.

Layered defense

Worship, protection creatures, Wrath, Wing Shards, and counters cover different attack patterns.

High white commitment

Pristine Angel, Silver Knight, Wrath, and Worship constrain mana and make Chrome Mox important.

Transformational sideboard

Exalted Angel and Rewind allow the deck to become a more conventional tap-out or permission control deck.

6 · Opening decisions

Mulligans and opening turns

Keep

  • Two or three lands with Matrix, Mana Leak, Silver Knight, or Wrath.
  • Chrome Mox hands that convert acceleration into a turn-two lock piece.
  • Against Affinity, hands with Matrix plus a way to survive creatures already in play.

Send back

  • Worship without a creature or protection plan.
  • Hands with only expensive Angels and no early interaction.
  • Colorless-heavy hands that cannot cast Wrath on time.

Opening priorities

  1. Determine whether Matrix, creature defense, or countermagic is the first priority.
  2. Against Affinity, play Matrix before they can use Ravager unless immediate combat requires Wrath.
  3. Sequence Worship only when a creature can remain in play through their likely removal.
7 · Rules and sequencing

Key interactions

Damping Matrix prevents activated abilities of artifacts and creatures unless they are mana abilities.

Worship changes lethal combat damage while you control a creature, but life loss from Disciple can still kill you.

March of the Machines makes artifact lands 0/0 creatures because they have mana value zero, so they die immediately.

Pristine Angel becomes protected while untapped and can be untapped by casting a spell.

8 · Play patterns

How to pilot the deck

  1. Recognize that Matrix does not remove the board; stabilize creatures before treating the lock as complete.
  2. Hold instants to untap Pristine Angel at the moment protection matters.
  3. Use March as removal against artifact lands, not merely as a win condition.
  4. After sideboarding, choose between the prison plan and a conventional Angel/counter plan rather than mixing both poorly.
9 · Post-board roles

Sideboarding principles

Affinity

Keep Matrix, March, Purge, and Wrath; trim slow card draw when necessary.

Goblins

Matrix is less dominant; prioritize Wrath, Wing Shards, and Angels.

Tooth and Nail

Bring Rewind and pressure; Matrix can stop Mindslaver but not Tooth.

Control

Transform into more threats and counters; Worship may be nearly blank.
