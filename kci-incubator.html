1 · Cover and deck identity

Krark-Clan Ironworks / Myr Incubator

KCI is a resource-conversion combo deck. Cheap artifacts and artifact lands accelerate the deck, draw cards, fix colors, and later become two colorless mana each through Krark-Clan Ironworks. Myr Incubator then turns most of the remaining library into a lethal board.

Unlike Affinity, the deck does not need its small artifacts to attack. This makes creature removal poor, but it also creates a dependency on a few expensive engine cards. Fabricate, Thirst for Knowledge, Thoughtcast, Serum Visions, and Condescend exist to assemble and protect those pieces.

Infrastructure becomes combo fuel

Every Talisman, Prism, Mox, and artifact land is useful before Ironworks and explosive after it.

High artifact density

Myr Incubator is stronger because the deck contains so few nonartifact cards and lands.

Compact alternate kills

Fireball and Goblin Charbelcher let excess mana win without waiting for Myr combat.

Transformational sideboard

Furnace Dragon and Pyroclasm allow the deck to punish Affinity and opponents who remove their creature interaction.

2 · What this deck does

The strategic machine

Turns 1–2: manufacture mana

Use Chrome Mox, Pentad Prism, Talismans, and artifact lands to jump from one mana to four or more.

Turns 2–3: locate the missing engine

Fabricate finds Ironworks or Incubator; blue cantrips filter; Thirst converts redundant artifacts into cards.

Combo turn: chain resources

Cast Ironworks, sacrifice artifacts for mana, cast Incubator, then activate it while enough artifacts remain in the library.

Finish or protect

Untap and attack with Myr, or use Fireball/Charbelcher. Condescend and post-board Mana Leak protect the critical turn.

3 · History and evolution

How the archetype reached Worlds

Mirrodin 2003

Myr Incubator offers a library-to-board engine

The six-mana artifact is initially expensive and awkward, but artifact lands make the token count enormous.

Fifth Dawn 2004

Ironworks and Pentad Prism create the mana chain

Krark-Clan Ironworks allows every artifact to become two mana; Pentad Prism and Serum Visions improve speed and consistency.

Early summer 2004

The archetype is actively “broken”

Contemporary deckbuilders explore Ironworks shells immediately, with Incubator recognized as one of the fastest payoffs.

Worlds 2004

Bevand posts a perfect Standard record

Manuel Bevand goes 6–0 and reaches the overall semifinals, demonstrating that the deck is not merely a fragile novelty.

Post-rotation

Gifts Ungiven changes the construction

Kamigawa-era lists use graveyard packages and different recursion, marking a second generation distinct from the Worlds Incubator build.

5 · Mulligans and opening turns

Keep a plan, not a collection of powerful cards

Strong keeps

  • Two mana sources plus a Prism/Talisman and either a tutor or draw spell.
  • Ironworks with enough artifacts to reach Incubator mana and a way to find Incubator.
  • Incubator plus Fabricate/draw and acceleration that realistically activates it by turn four.
  • Against control, a hand with Condescend or post-board Mana Leak plus a credible combo clock.

Common mulligans

  • Hands with Incubator and Charbelcher but no acceleration.
  • Artifact-dense hands that produce mana but never find Ironworks, Incubator, Fabricate, or draw.
  • One-land hands whose Chrome Mox would require imprinting the only blue selection spell.
  • Post-board reactive hands that cannot present a combo before the opponent’s inevitability.

Fast comboArtifact land into Mox/Prism, then Ironworks and a tutor. Count mana after each possible sacrifice before choosing the line.

Against AffinityPreserve Pyroclasm/Furnace Dragon sequencing while maintaining enough artifacts to combo.
6 · Key interactions

Rules and sequencing that define the deck

Ironworks sacrifices an artifact as a cost, so the opponent cannot destroy that artifact in response to prevent the two mana.

Artifact lands can tap for mana and then be sacrificed to Ironworks during the same combo turn.

Pentad Prism may spend its charge counters before being sacrificed, effectively producing several mana across the turn.

Myr Incubator exiles artifact cards from the library as part of its resolution and creates that many Myr tokens.

Fabricate can find either half of the core engine, Goblin Charbelcher, or a sideboard artifact plan.

Condescend scales with the huge mana engine and scry helps locate the next combo piece.

A resolved and activated Incubator can leave the library extremely small; know whether the game ends before the next draw step.

7 · How to pilot the deck

Decision rules for real games

Write out the mana chain mentally before casting Ironworks. The key question is not current mana, but total mana after every artifact has served its first function.

Use colored mana before sacrificing colored sources. Talismans and artifact lands may be the only way to cast Fabricate, Thirst, or Fireball.

Do not expose Incubator when activation cannot occur unless it is necessary to draw out a counter.

Against artifact removal, sacrifice the targeted permanent to Ironworks whenever its battlefield function is no longer needed.

Count the number of artifact cards remaining before activating Incubator; a small token count may require Fireball or another turn.

Use Condescend proactively on the opponent’s most disruptive tempo play, not solely as ceremonial combo protection.

After sideboarding, decide whether you remain pure combo or become a Furnace Dragon control-combo deck. Splitting the plans halfway creates weak draws.

8 · Sideboarding principles

Change roles without dismantling the engine

Mana Leak replaces slower cards when stack interaction is more important than raw speed.

Pyroclasm is for Affinity, Goblins, and opposing token plans; preserve your artifact mana through the sweep.

Furnace Dragon is a transformational trump against artifact-heavy decks and can be cast cheaply from the same infrastructure.

Avoid bringing in so many colored cards that the artifact-land mana base no longer casts the deck consistently.

Against dedicated artifact hate, value cards such as Thirst and Thoughtcast become more important because every engine piece may trade one-for-one.

10 · Historical notes and sources

What the archetype teaches

KCI demonstrates a classic combo-design principle: the deck is strongest when setup cards are not dead setup. Its mana rocks and artifact lands perform normal work, then become combo resources without requiring extra cards.
