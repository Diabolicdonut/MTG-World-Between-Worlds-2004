Distributed milestone

Regionals as the format laboratory

Regionals was not one tournament but a network of large qualifying events. Together they revealed which Darksteel and Skullclamp strategies were real before the international Grand Prix and Nationals season.

Archive principle: A deck earns a dossier when it changes how the season is understood, not only when it later appears in the Worlds Top 8.
Metagame

What Regionals established

Ravager Affinity

The baseline threat and the reason every deck needed maindeck or sideboard artifact interaction.

Clamp Goblins

A tribal deck capable of matching Affinity’s card flow through expendable bodies.

Tooth, Slide, and control

Slow decks survived only by dedicating enormous space to artifacts and creature swarms.

Cemetery Cloud

A new B/G engine that turned Skullclamp, Cemetery, utility creatures, and Death Cloud into a coherent resource plan.

Season impact

What carried forward

Affinity and Goblins proceeded to Grand Prix Brussels, Elf and Nail and other Clamp engines peaked at U.S. Nationals, and Death Cloud remained a real post-ban strategy at Grand Prix Nagoya. Regionals therefore functions as the first broad map of the Skullclamp metagame.
