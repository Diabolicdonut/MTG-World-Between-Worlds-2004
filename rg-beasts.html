Red-Green Beasts

Large, durable creatures and repeatable artifact destruction challenge Affinity without becoming a pile of narrow hate cards.

Scope

Scope of this guide

Year position: The deck belongs to the Skullclamp-era fair field and is represented by Michael Aitchison’s U.S. Nationals Top 8 list.
1 · Deck identity

Red-Green Beasts

R/G Beasts is a metagame midrange deck: nearly every creature either accelerates, destroys artifacts, gains life, or outclasses small attackers. Contested Cliffs turns the Beast creature type into repeatable removal.

2 · Game plan

What this deck does

Accelerate with Birds and Vine Trellis.

Use Viridian Shaman and Oxidize to break Affinity’s mana and threat density.

Stabilize with Ravenous Baloth and Starstorm.

Close with Molder Slug, Arc-Slogger, or repeated Contested Cliffs fights.

3 · Development

History and evolution

Mirrodin enters Standard

Artifacts reward green creature decks

Viridian Shaman and Molder Slug are threats and hate cards simultaneously.

Darksteel

Affinity accelerates

The deck increases cheap removal and relies on Baloth/Starstorm to survive explosive turns.

U.S. Nationals

Aitchison reaches Top 8

The strategy demonstrates that fair midrange can succeed when every card is selected for the central matchup.

After Skullclamp

The shell loses relative purpose

Other control decks gain Fifth Dawn tools while R/G remains vulnerable to Wrath and Tooth.

4 · Construction

Key design characteristics

Threat-hate overlap

Shaman and Slug advance the board while attacking artifacts.

Creature sizing

Baloth and Arc-Slogger survive Pyroclasm and dominate Goblin-sized combat.

Contested Cliffs

The land converts Beasts into removal without spending cards.

Limited card draw

The deck must make each threat matter; it cannot recover as easily as Clamp or Witness engines.

6 · Opening decisions

Mulligans and opening turns

Keep

  • Green mana plus Birds/Trellis and a three- or four-mana threat.
  • Hands with Oxidize/Shaman against Affinity.
  • Balanced hands that can cast both green creatures and red removal.

Send back

  • Slow hands with only five-mana creatures.
  • Hands whose mana cannot support Starstorm or Arc-Slogger.
  • Reactive hands with no threat to capitalize on artifact removal.

Opening priorities

  1. Develop mana first unless an immediate Affinity permanent must die.
  2. Choose whether Shaman should hit an artifact land, Skullclamp, or threat based on the opponent’s bottleneck.
  3. Preserve Baloth sacrifice value when removal or racing makes life relevant.
7 · Rules and sequencing

Key interactions

Molder Slug forces every player to sacrifice an artifact each upkeep, including its controller.

Contested Cliffs requires a Beast and causes a fight-like damage exchange using power.

Ravenous Baloth can sacrifice itself or another Beast for life.

Grab the Reins can steal a creature or sacrifice one for damage when entwined.

8 · Play patterns

How to pilot the deck

  1. Attack the opponent’s mana when artifact lands are their bottleneck; attack Ravager/Clamp when engine pieces are the bottleneck.
  2. Do not treat Birds as disposable if the hand needs double red for Slogger or Starstorm.
  3. Use life total as a resource until Baloth can swing the race.
  4. Against sweepers, deploy one high-impact creature at a time.
9 · Post-board roles

Sideboarding principles

Affinity

Maximize Naturalize, Detonate, and Pyroclasm; trim slow cards that do not affect Plating or Disciple.

Control

Bring Grab the Reins and durable threats; avoid drawing too many artifact-only cards.

Goblins

Starstorm, Pyroclasm, Baloth, and Slogger control the board.

Tooth

Pressure mana and race; the deck has little stack interaction.
