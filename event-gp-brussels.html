2004 Standard environment guide

Grand Prix Brussels 2004

The 734-player peak of Skullclamp Standard: Affinity wins, Goblins occupies four Top 8 seats, and Matrix control and land destruction fight back.

Table of contentsEvent overviewTop finishersExact decklistsHistorical meaning
1 · Event

Event overview

Grand Prix Brussels on May 30 was the clearest public demonstration that Darksteel had transformed Standard. Tobias Henke defeated Kai Budde in an Affinity final; six of eight decks were Affinity or Goblins.

Historical meaning
